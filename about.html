About PowerNest Electric

Reliable Power. Better Future.

PowerNest Electric is an electrical and energy solutions company focused on providing dependable services for homes, businesses, industries, and other customers in Liberia.

Our services cover electrical installation, wiring, maintenance, repairs, troubleshooting, solar and energy solutions.

We believe that reliable electrical systems are an important part of comfortable homes, productive businesses, safe workplaces, and stronger communities.

Our goal is to provide practical electrical solutions while building strong relationships with our customers through communication, professionalism, safety, and quality.
